Home Page

Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC), a progressive pre-K to 12 co-educational school in the Philippines. The school uses Dr. Maria Montessori's holistic method, blending tradition and innovation to foster self-developing and productive individuals.

  • Programs Offered:
    • Casa (Pre-Kindergarten to Kindergarten, ages 3–5)
    • Primary / Elementary (Grades 1–3, ages 6–8)
    • Intermediate (Grades 4–6)
    • Junior High School (Grades 7–10)
    • Senior High School (Grades 11–12)
  • Campuses:
    • Angeles (Pampanga)
    • Fairview (Quezon City)
    • Greenhills (San Juan City)
    • Las Piñas (Metro Manila)
    • Sta. Ana (Manila heritage district)
